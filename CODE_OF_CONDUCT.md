# Agorail Labs community code of conduct

Agorail Labs values rigorous technical debate, verifiable claims, and respectful collaboration. Disagreement—including pointed criticism of a specification—is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Expected conduct

- Focus on designs, evidence, reproducible failures, and trade-offs rather than motives or personal characteristics.
- Treat contributors and reviewers with respect regardless of experience, background, or affiliation.
- Correct errors openly, including overstated evidence and unsupported claims.
- Respect privacy, confidential disclosures, licensing, and contributors' boundaries.
- Avoid spam, harassment, discriminatory abuse, intimidation, or unwanted disclosure of personal information.

These expectations apply to the organization's repositories, pull requests, issues, and discussions.

## Review and moderation

Maintainers may ask for clarification, edit or hide inappropriate material where GitHub permits, lock conversations, decline contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep discussions constructive. Technical disagreement by itself is not misconduct.

For a conduct concern, use a **private contact method listed on the maintainers' GitHub profiles**, rather than posting sensitive or identifying allegations in a public issue. Include relevant links and context while minimizing personal information. If no private contact option is available, do not publish confidential details just to reach a maintainer.

No fixed response-time guarantee is offered. Decisions will consider the available evidence and safety of affected participants. This policy does not replace GitHub's own site-wide rules or abuse-reporting mechanisms.
